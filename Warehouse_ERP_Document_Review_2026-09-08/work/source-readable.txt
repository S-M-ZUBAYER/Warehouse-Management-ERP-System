[2] A. Dashboard Basics
[3] Q-001: What is the Dashboard?
[4] Answer: The Dashboard is the main summary page of the ERP. It gives you a quick view of products, orders, inventory activity, sales trends, and store subscriptions.
[5] You can see the process through the app screenshots https://printernoble.com/chatbot/uploads/1788406865_WERP_Q-01.png
[6] Q-002: Why should I use the Dashboard?
[7] Answer: It helps you understand the overall condition of your business without opening every section separately.
[8] You can quickly identify:
[9] Order activity
[10] Inventory movement
[11] Low-stock products
[12] Out-of-stock products
[13] Sales patterns
[14] Subscription issues
[15] Q-003: Does opening the Dashboard change any business data?
[16] Answer: No. Viewing the Dashboard does not add, edit, or delete products, orders, or inventory.
[17] Q-004: How do I return to the Dashboard?
[18] Answer: Click Dashboard in the main sidebar.
[19] Q-005: Why does the Dashboard take time to load?
[20] Answer: The ERP may be collecting information from products, orders, warehouses, and connected marketplace stores.
[21] Loading can take longer when:
[22] The internet connection is slow.
[23] A marketplace is responding slowly.
[24] The account contains many records.
[25] The page is synchronizing information.
[26] Q-006: Why is some Dashboard information empty?
[27] Answer: Possible reasons include:
[28] No marketplace store is connected.
[29] No products have been added or synchronized.
[30] No inventory has been received.
[31] No orders exist for the selected period.
[32] The account is new.
[33] A filter excludes the available information.
[34] Synchronization has not completed.
[35] Q-007: Does an empty Dashboard mean the ERP is broken?
[36] Answer: No. A new account can display empty charts and summaries until stores, products, inventory, and orders are added.
[37] Q-008: Does the Dashboard update automatically?
[38] Answer: Some information may refresh when the ERP receives new product, inventory, or order information.
[39] If something appears outdated, refresh the page and check the relevant detailed section.
