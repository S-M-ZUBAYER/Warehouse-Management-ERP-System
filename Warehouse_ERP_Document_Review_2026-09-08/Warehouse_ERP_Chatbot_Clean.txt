Warehouse ERP Customer Questions and Answers
Q-001: What is the Dashboard?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
The Dashboard is the main summary page of the ERP. It gives you a quick view of products, orders, inventory activity, sales trends, and store subscriptions.

Q-002: Why should I use the Dashboard?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
It helps you understand the overall condition of your business without opening every section separately.
You can quickly identify:
- Order activity
- Inventory movement
- Low-stock products
- Out-of-stock products
- Sales patterns
- Subscription issues

Q-003: Does opening the Dashboard change any business data?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
No. Viewing the Dashboard does not add, edit, or delete products, orders, or inventory.

Q-004: How do I return to the Dashboard?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Click Dashboard in the main sidebar.

Q-005: Why does the Dashboard take time to load?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Dashboard loads summary information from your company records. A slow connection, a busy service, or a large result can delay the page. Wait for the loading state to finish, then reload once if it remains stuck. Contact support with the error if the summary still does not load.

Q-006: Why is some Dashboard information empty?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Possible reasons include:
No marketplace store is connected.
No products have been added or synchronized.
No inventory has been received.
No orders exist for the selected period.
The account is new.
A filter excludes the available information.
Synchronization has not completed.

Q-007: Does an empty Dashboard mean the ERP is broken?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
No. A new account can display empty charts and summaries until stores, products, inventory, and orders are added.

Q-008: Does the Dashboard update automatically?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
The Dashboard loads information when you open it. Changing a chart period or platform loads that selection. To check for newer information, reload the page and wait for loading to finish. It does not provide a continuously updating live feed.

Q-009: What are the main ERP sections shown in the sidebar?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
The sidebar contains Dashboard, Product Management, Inventory Management, Order Management, Warehouse Management, System Configuration, Help Center, and Contact. Expand a section to see its pages. A Sub Account may see fewer pages because of its assigned role.

Q-010: What is Product Management?
Answer: Location: Product Management
Product Management contains the product catalog and combined-product bundles.

Q-011: What is Inventory Management?
Answer: Location: Inventory Management
Inventory Management controls Merchant SKUs, SKU mappings, warehouse quantities, inbound stock, outbound stock, and inventory history.

Q-012: What is Order Management?
Answer: Location: Order Management
Order Management controls order processing, shipping stages, returns, cancellations, and manual orders.

Q-013: What is Warehouse Management?
Answer: Location: Warehouse Management
Warehouse Management controls the warehouses or inventory locations used by the ERP.

Q-014: What is System Configuration?
Answer: Location: System Configuration
System Configuration manages store connections, Sub Accounts, roles, and permissions.

Q-015: How do I open a submenu?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Click a section such as Product Management or Inventory Management.
The related pages should appear below it.

Q-016: How do I hide the sidebar?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Use the Collapse sidebar button near the ERP logo.
This only changes the screen layout.

Q-017: What is the Overview section?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Overview provides quick summary cards for important product, order, and stock conditions.

Q-018: What does Total Platform Products mean?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Total Platform Products counts marketplace product listings saved for your company. A product with several variations is not necessarily counted once per variation. Click the card to open Inventory Management > SKU Mapping > By Product and inspect the listings.

Q-019: Why might Total Platform Products differ from Merchant SKUs?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
A marketplace product and a Merchant SKU are not the same thing.
Platform product means a marketplace listing.
Merchant SKU means the internal inventory identity.
Several marketplace listings can be connected to one Merchant SKU.

Q-020: What does Today's Orders mean?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Today's Orders counts distinct marketplace orders recorded in the ERP's sales records for today. It is not a count of every order type in the system. Click the card to open Order Management > Order Processing > All Order with today's date filter.

Q-021: Why might Today's Orders differ from the marketplace?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Possible reasons include:
- Synchronization delay
- Different timezone
- Store filter differences
- Cancelled orders
- Manual orders
- Marketplace processing delay

Q-022: What timezone is used for Today's Orders?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
The daily summary uses the ERP server's day boundary. The Dashboard does not show the server timezone. If a date differs from your marketplace report, compare the same store and period and ask support to confirm the timezone used by your hosted ERP.

Q-023: What does Low Stock mean?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Low Stock counts warehouse stock records with a quantity above zero and at or below their minimum-stock warning level. Click Low Stock to open Inventory Management > Inventory List with the low-stock filter applied.

Q-024: How is the low-stock level decided?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Go to Product Management > Product List. Select the products, open Bulk Action, and choose Batch Stock Alert Setting. Enter Set minimum Stock, then click Set. You can also set a warning under Inventory Management > Inventory List > Bulk Action > Stock alert settings. Setting a warning does not add or remove stock.

Q-025: Does Low Stock mean the product cannot be sold?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
No. It usually means the product still has stock but is approaching its warning limit.

Q-026: What does Out of Stock mean?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Out of Stock counts warehouse stock records whose recorded quantity is zero or below. Click the card to open Inventory Management > Inventory List with the out-of-stock filter. A separate Lock Quantity can reduce available stock even when Quantity is above zero.

Q-027: Why might a product appear out of stock when physical stock exists?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Possible reasons include:
Stock was not received in the ERP.
The wrong warehouse is being checked.
SKU mapping is missing.
Inventory synchronization failed.
Stock is locked for active orders.
The physical count has not been recorded.
The product is connected to the wrong Merchant SKU.

Q-028: Can I click an Overview card?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Yes. Total Platform Products opens Inventory Management > SKU Mapping > By Product. Today's Orders opens Order Management > Order Processing > All Order for today. Low Stock and Out of Stock open Inventory Management > Inventory List with the corresponding stock filter.

Q-029: Where should I investigate an incorrect product total?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Click Total Platform Products on the Dashboard, or go to Inventory Management > SKU Mapping > By Product. Check Select Platform, Select Shop, and the product search. Product Management > Product List contains internal Merchant SKU records, so its total can legitimately differ.

Q-030: Where should I investigate incorrect stock information?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Go to Inventory Management > Inventory List and select the affected warehouse. Search for the SKU and compare Quantity with Lock Quantity. Then use Inventory Management > Inventory Log to check stock movements, and SKU Mapping > By Product to check the marketplace connection.

Q-031: What is the Inventory Status chart?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Inventory Status shows daily Stock-In and Stock-Out activity for a selected month.

Q-032: What does Stock-In mean?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Stock-In represents inventory added to a warehouse.
It can include completed inbound or manual receiving transactions.

Q-033: What does Stock-Out mean?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Stock-Out represents inventory removed from a warehouse.
It can include order fulfillment, outbound operations, or other stock deductions.

Q-034: How do I change the Inventory Status period?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Use the year and month selectors above the chart.
Changing the reporting period does not change inventory records.

Q-035: Why does the chart show dates across the bottom?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
The horizontal axis represents days in the selected month.
Some labels may be skipped to keep the chart readable.

Q-036: Why does the Inventory Status chart show no activity?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Possible reasons include:
No stock moved during the selected period.
Inbound records are still drafts.
Outbound records are not completed.
No warehouse inventory exists.
The wrong period is selected.
The page has not finished loading.

Q-037: Does Stock-In include inventory that is still On The Way?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
No. Inbound > On The Way represents stock expected at the warehouse. Stock-In records an actual positive stock movement, such as a confirmed receipt. Use Inventory Management > Inbound > On The Way > Receive after the goods arrive.

Q-038: Where can I see the reason for a stock movement?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Go to:
- Inventory Management - Inventory Log
The log includes the movement purpose, quantity, remaining quantity, and operation time.

Q-039: Can I filter the Dashboard inventory chart by warehouse?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Dashboard > Inventory Status does not have a warehouse selector. To investigate one warehouse, go to Inventory Management > Inventory List or Inventory Log and select that warehouse. Use the Dashboard chart for the combined activity it displays.

Q-040: What is the Order Status section?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Order Status summarizes orders by their current processing stage for a selected date range.

Q-041: What does Total mean in Order Status?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Total represents the combined orders included in the selected date range and status summary.

Q-042: What are Pending Orders?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Pending Orders are waiting for an initial action or marketplace-processing result.

Q-043: What are Processing Orders?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Processing Orders are being prepared, packed, pushed, or moved through the fulfillment workflow.

Q-044: What are Shipped Orders?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Shipped Orders have been dispatched but are not yet considered completed.

Q-045: What are Completed Orders?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Completed Orders have reached the final normal fulfillment stage.
Depending on the marketplace, completion may mean delivered, buyer-confirmed, or finalized.

Q-046: What are Cancelled Orders?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Cancelled Orders are orders whose normal fulfillment process was stopped.

Q-047: How do I change the Order Status period?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Use the displayed date-range control in the Order Status section.
Choose the period you want to review.

Q-048: Does changing the date range modify orders?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
No. It only changes which orders are included in the summary.

Q-049: Can I click an order-status category?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Yes. Click a category in Dashboard > Order Status to open its related order view. The selected date range is carried into the order view. Check the destination filters before comparing its count with the Dashboard.

Q-050: Why does the order total differ from the sum I expected?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Possible reasons include:
- Date filters
- Different status definitions
- Marketplace synchronization delays
- Returns
- Cancellations
- Manual orders
- Orders changing status during the review

Q-051: Where can I find a specific order?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Go to Order Management > Order Processing > All Order. Select the correct platform, store, date range, and search type, then enter the order number and click Search. For orders created directly in the ERP, also check Order Management > Manual Order or Platform Manual Order.

Q-052: Why is an order missing from the expected status?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
It may have:
- Moved to another status
- Been cancelled
- Entered a return workflow
- Failed to synchronize
- Been created as a Manual Order
- Fallen outside the selected date range

Q-053: What is the Sales Trends chart?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Sales Trends shows daily sales activity for a selected platform, month, and year.

Q-054: What can the Sales Trends chart help me understand?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
It can help identify:
- Busy sales days
- Slower periods
- Seasonal patterns
- Marketplace differences
- Changes in daily activity

Q-055: How do I change the sales period?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Use the year and month selectors above the Sales Trends chart.

Q-056: How do I compare marketplace performance?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Use the platform selector.
When stores are connected, supported platform choices may become available.

Q-057: What does All mean in the platform selector?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
All combines sales activity from every available supported platform included in the Dashboard.

Q-058: Why does the platform selector only show All?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Possible reasons include:
No marketplace store is connected.
Store authorization is incomplete.
Connected platforms have not loaded.
The account has no eligible platform data.

Q-059: Does Sales Trends show revenue or order quantity?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Sales Trends normally plots sales value. If a day has no positive sales value, the chart can display quantity or order count instead. Hover over a point and read Sales, Orders, Qty, and any Graph label to identify the value shown. Use the marketplace settlement report to confirm payments; this chart is not a profit report.

Q-060: Why might Sales Trends differ from Today's Orders?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
They may use different rules.
For example:
Today's Orders may count newly created orders.
Sales Trends may use another status or measurement.
Cancelled or returned orders may be treated differently.
Synchronization can occur at different times.

Q-061: Why might Sales Trends differ from a marketplace report?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Possible reasons include:
- Different date ranges
- Different timezones
- Cancelled or returned orders
- Platform processing delays
- Manual orders
- Different sales definitions
- Store authorization problems

Q-062: Can I use Sales Trends as an accounting report?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Not by itself.
Accounting requires confirmed revenue, costs, fees, refunds, shipping, taxes, and other financial information.

Q-063: What is Store Plans?
Answer: Location: Sidebar > Store Plans
Store Plans shows subscription information for marketplace stores connected to the ERP.

Q-064: Why does Store Plans say that no stores were found?
Answer: Location: Sidebar > Store Plans
Possible reasons include:
No store has been authorized.
Store authorization is incomplete.
Stores are connected to another account.
The store list has not loaded.
The account is new.

Q-065: How do I connect a marketplace store?
Answer: Location: Sidebar > Store Plans
1. Open System Configuration > Store Authorization.
2. Click Add Store.
3. Choose the platform and complete the fields shown for your store and country.
4. Continue to the marketplace authorization screen and approve the connection for the correct store.
5. Return to Store Authorization and check the authorization status. A subscription and store authorization are separate requirements.

Q-066: Where does See all in Store Plans take me?
Answer: Location: Sidebar > Store Plans
In the sidebar's Store Plans area, See all opens System Configuration > Store Authorization. Use the Authorized Store List there to review connected stores, current plans, and remaining days.

Q-067: What does Upgrade Plan do?
Answer: Location: Sidebar > Store Plans
It opens the subscription-selection page.
Opening it does not complete a purchase.

Q-068: When should I buy a subscription?
Answer: Location: Sidebar > Store Plans
Consider a subscription after confirming that:
Your marketplace is supported.
The correct stores can be connected.
Product synchronization works.
SKU mapping fits your catalog.
Inventory workflows meet your needs.
Order processing fits your team.
The final cost is approved.

Q-069: What happens when a store subscription expires?
Answer: Location: Sidebar > Store Plans
Marketplace-related operations may be limited or interrupted.
Existing warehouse records should not automatically be assumed to be deleted.

Q-070: Does renewing a plan reconnect an unauthorized store?
Answer: Location: Sidebar > Store Plans
Not necessarily.
Subscription and store authorization are separate. Both may need to be valid.

Q-071: How do I change the ERP language?
Answer: Location: Top bar > Select language
Use the Select language dropdown at the top of the page.

Q-072: Which languages are available?

Q-073: Will changing the language translate product and customer data?
Answer: Location: Top bar > Select language
No. It mainly changes built-in interface labels.
Imported product names, store names, addresses, and customer information may remain in their original language.

Q-074: What is the user button in the upper-right corner?
Answer: Location: Top bar and account menu
Click your account name or picture in the top-right corner to open the account menu. It contains Profile Info, My gift, Share referral code, and Log Out. Profile Info displays account details; Log Out ends your current sign-in.

Q-075: What is Profile Info?
Answer: Location: Top-right account menu > Profile Info
Click your account name or picture in the top-right corner, then choose Profile Info. This window displays your signed-in account details, such as name, email, role, and assigned access. It has a Close button; it does not provide a profile-edit or Save action.

Q-076: What is My gift?
Answer: Location: Top-right account menu > My gift
My gift shows rewards assigned to your account, including any address or receipt action still required. Open the top-right account menu > My gift to view them. This is separate from Order Management > Manual Order > Add Gift, which creates a customer gift order.

Q-077: What does Share referral code do?
Answer: Location: Top-right account menu > Share referral code
Open the top-right account menu > Share referral code. When your available code appears, click Copy beside it. You can then paste it into a message to the person you want to invite. Opening the window alone does not copy the code.

Q-078: What does Log Out do?
Answer: Location: Top-right account menu > Log Out
It ends the current signed-in session.
It does not delete the account or business records.

Q-079: How can the Dashboard help me run my business?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
It brings the most important operational signals into one place so you can identify stock, order, sales, and subscription issues quickly.

Q-080: What should I check first every morning?

A useful routine is:
Review Today's Orders.

Review Low Stock.
Review Out of Stock.
Check inventory movement.
Look for unusual sales changes.
Check store subscription warnings.

Q-081: What should I review before purchasing new inventory?

Check:
- Low-stock products

Q-082: How can the Dashboard help prevent missed orders?

It highlights order activity and processing stages.
Staff can open the relevant Order Management page when pending or processing orders require attention.

Q-083: How can it help prevent overselling?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
The Dashboard identifies low-stock and out-of-stock conditions, while the detailed inventory pages provide quantities, locks, mappings, and synchronization status.

Q-084: Can the Dashboard replace detailed reports?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
No. It provides a summary.
Use the detailed product, inventory, order, warehouse, and configuration pages when investigating a problem or making an important decision.

Q-085: How can I know whether the ERP is helping my business?

Track changes in:

Q-086: What should I do before relying on the Dashboard?
Answer: Location: Dashboard
Confirm:
Stores are authorized.
Products are synchronized.
Merchant SKUs are created.
SKU mappings are correct.
Warehouses are configured.
Inventory is accurate.
Staff roles are properly assigned.

Q-087: What is Product Management?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Product Management is where you can view and manage products used by the ERP.
It contains:
- Product List
- Combine SKU

Q-088: How do I open Product Management?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Click Product Management in the main sidebar.
The Product List and Combine SKU pages should appear underneath it.

Q-089: What is the difference between Product List and Combine SKU?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Product List manages your internal Merchant SKU product records, including names, images, dimensions, and inventory information. Combine SKU defines bundles made from those Merchant SKUs. To see imported marketplace listings, go to Inventory Management > SKU Mapping > By Product.

Q-090: Does opening Product Management change anything?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
No. Viewing, searching, filtering, and changing pages do not normally modify product information.

Q-091: Which Product Management actions can change data?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
In Product Management > Product List, Add Single Product, Add Product via Template, Edit, Batch Stock Alert Setting, Delete, and Batch Delete can save changes. In Product Management > Combine SKU, saving Add Combine SKU or confirming Delete changes bundle records. Details, search, and filtering are for viewing.

Q-092: Who should use Product Management?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
It is useful for:
- Product administrators
- Inventory managers
- Store managers
- Warehouse managers
- Authorized business owners

Q-093: What is the Product List?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Product Management > Product List shows the ERP Merchant SKU product records. Use it to add or edit a product, review its image and dimensions, and check warehouse availability. Marketplace listings and their connections appear under Inventory Management > SKU Mapping > By Product.

Q-094: What information appears in the Product List?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
The table includes:
- Select All
- Image
- SKU
- Product Name
- Available in Inventory
- In-transit Inventory
- Details
- Actions

Q-095: What does SKU mean?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
SKU means Stock Keeping Unit.
It is a code used to identify a particular product or product variation.

Q-096: Is a SKU the same as a product name?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
No.
SKU is a relatively short product identifier.
Product Name is the readable description of the product.

Q-097: Can different product variations have different SKUs?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Yes. Different colors, sizes, models, packs, or other variations should normally have separate SKUs.

Q-098: Why is a unique SKU important?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
A unique SKU helps the ERP:
- Identify the correct product
- Map marketplace listings
- Track inventory
- Process orders
- Prevent stock mistakes
- Investigate inventory movements

Q-099: Can two products use the same SKU?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
They should not use the same SKU if they represent different physical items.
Duplicate SKUs can cause incorrect inventory and order fulfillment.

Q-100: What is the difference between a platform SKU and a Merchant SKU?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Platform SKU comes from a marketplace listing.
Merchant SKU is the ERP's internal inventory identity.
SKU Mapping connects them.

Q-101: Why might the product name be in another language?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Product information may be imported from its marketplace listing.
Changing the ERP interface language does not automatically translate imported product names.

Q-102: What does Select Warehouse do?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
It filters products according to a selected warehouse.
This helps you focus on products associated with one inventory location.

Q-103: Why does the warehouse selector show a placeholder?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
A placeholder means no warehouse has been selected or no warehouse is currently available.
If no warehouse exists, create one under Warehouse Management before using warehouse-based product functions.

Q-104: What does Product Status do?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
It filters products by their current status.
The available status options may depend on the product source and ERP configuration.

Q-105: What does Select Country do?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
It filters products according to a country or marketplace region.
This is useful when the business operates in several countries.

Q-106: What is the dedicated SKU field?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
It is used to find a product by its SKU.
Enter the complete SKU for the most precise result.

Q-107: What is the general Search field?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Use Search on Product Management > Product List to find the product, and use the SKU field when you know its code. If a search finds nothing, clear the SKU field and other filters before trying the product name. An entered SKU can take priority over the general search.

Q-108: Can I use several filters together?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
You can combine Warehouse, Product Status, and Country with a search. Use either the SKU field or general Search for the item you want; an entered SKU takes priority over general Search. Click Clear all to reset the filters.

Q-109: Why does no product appear after I apply filters?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Clear the active filters and check the SKU spelling and warehouse. If the dedicated SKU field is filled, clear it before using the general product search. Product List contains Merchant SKU records; look in Inventory Management > SKU Mapping > By Product for imported marketplace listings instead.

Q-110: Why is the Product List empty?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Possible reasons include:
The account is new.
No marketplace store is connected.
No products have been added.
Product synchronization has not occurred.
The selected filters exclude all products.
Store authorization is incomplete.

Q-111: How do I get marketplace products into the ERP?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
1. Go to System Configuration > Store Authorization and authorize the store.
2. Open Inventory Management > SKU Mapping > By Product.
3. Click Sync Product, select the platform and store, and click Confirm Sync.
4. Expand Show child SKUs to inspect variations.
5. Generate or map the required Merchant SKUs, then check their stock in Inventory Management > Inventory List.

Q-112: What does the Image column show in Product List?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
It displays the image associated with the product.
The image may come from the marketplace listing or an ERP product record.

Q-113: Why is a product image missing?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Possible reasons include:
The original listing has no image.
Image synchronization failed.
The source image is unavailable.
The product record is incomplete.
The internet connection is slow.
The image has not finished loading.

Q-114: Where can I add a product image?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Go to Product Management > Product List. For a new product, choose Add Products > Add Single Product, then Browse File. For an existing product, open Actions > Edit and choose its image. Use a JPEG or PNG smaller than 5 MB, then click Save.

Q-115: Will changing an ERP image update the marketplace?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Changing a product image through Product Management > Product List updates the ERP Merchant SKU record. This form does not publish an image update to a connected marketplace listing. Change the listing image in the marketplace seller tools when that is required.

Q-116: What kind of product image should I use?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Use an image that:
- Clearly shows the correct product
- Matches the exact variation
- Does not include unrelated items
- Meets marketplace image requirements
- Uses an appropriate size and file format

Q-117: What does Available in Inventory mean?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Available in Inventory is the recorded stock on hand minus stock reserved for orders, with a minimum displayed value of zero. For example, Quantity 20 and Lock Quantity 5 leave 15 available. Check the warehouse before comparing this with a physical count.

Q-118: What does In-transit Inventory mean?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
It represents stock moving toward a warehouse or waiting for the inbound process to finish.

Q-119: Is in-transit inventory available for immediate sale?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
No. In-transit inventory is expected stock, not stock already received and available. When goods arrive, record the actual received quantity through Inventory Management > Inbound > On The Way > Receive.

Q-120: Why does a product show no available inventory?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Possible reasons include:
No stock has been received.
The product is not mapped to a Merchant SKU.
Stock belongs to another warehouse.
Inventory is locked for active orders.
Inventory synchronization failed.
An inbound transaction is not completed.

Q-121: Why might the physical stock and ERP stock differ?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Possible reasons include:
Receiving was not recorded.
Outbound stock was not recorded.
An incorrect warehouse was used.
An order reserved inventory.
SKU mapping is incorrect.
Synchronization is delayed.
A physical counting error occurred.

Q-122: Where should I investigate an incorrect quantity?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Open Product Management > Product List > Details for the affected product. Then go to Inventory Management > Inventory List, select the warehouse, and compare Quantity and Lock Quantity. Use Inventory Management > Inventory Log to trace receipts, outbound shipments, sales deductions, and adjustments.

Q-123: Does Product Management directly control warehouse stock?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Product Management displays inventory-related information, but stock movements are mainly managed through Inventory Management.

Q-124: What does the checkbox in each row do?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
It selects that product for a possible batch operation.
Selecting a checkbox does not change the product.

Q-125: What does Select All do in Product List?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Select All selects the products matching the current filters across all result pages. Check the selected count before using Bulk Action. Clear the selection and tick individual rows if you only want specific products.

Q-126: Why is Bulk Action disabled?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Bulk Action remains disabled until at least one product is selected.

Q-127: Which bulk actions are available?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
The Product List provides:
- Batch Print
- Batch Export
- Batch Stock Alert Setting
- Batch Delete

Q-128: What does Batch Print do?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List
Select the products in Product Management > Product List, then choose Bulk Action > Batch Print. A print view opens for the selected records. Check the preview, choose your printer, and print. If no print window appears, check whether your browser blocked it.

Q-129: What does Batch Export do?
Answer: Location: Product Management > Product List

3. Click Top Up and review the payment page.
4. Complete the payment, return to the ERP, and check the wallet balance before booking.
Wallet credit is added after payment processing and the currency-conversion reserve, so do not assume it equals the gross payment amount.

Q-889: Why does Shipping Wallet say I need more MYR before booking?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
The available wallet balance is below the selected courier charge after conversion to MYR. Check the displayed shortfall and use Top Up if you want that courier service. Recheck the credited balance before Submit Order. Creating an order without courier is a separate delivery choice, not a way to confirm an unpaid courier booking.

Q-890: Is Create Without Courier free of every ERP requirement?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
Create Without Courier does not charge the Shipping Wallet for a courier booking, because you arrange delivery yourself. It still creates an order and can deduct stock. It also does not bypass company plan or trial requirements; follow Purchase Plan Required if the ERP shows that message.

Q-891: How do I add tracking information after creating an order without courier?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
Find the self-arranged order in Manual Order List and open its three-dot Action menu > Edit. In Update Delivery Information, enter the courier and tracking details and add the waybill URL or supported PDF when available. Review the information and click Save. This records your delivery details; it does not create a courier booking.

Q-892: Does Add Gift add a free item to the order I am already viewing?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
Add Gift opens a separate gift-type Manual Order workflow. It does not simply insert an extra item into an existing order. Select the correct recipient, warehouse, products, and quantities, and review the new order carefully so you do not send or deduct the same gift twice.

Q-893: Does cancelling an EasyParcel shipment restore stock or refund Shipping Wallet?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
The Manual Order Cancel action requests shipment cancellation. It does not automatically restore the deducted stock or refund the Shipping Wallet balance. Check the cancellation result, physical stock, and payment or wallet records separately, and ask support about any required correction or refund.

Q-894: Can I use Manual Order for an international EasyParcel shipment?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
The current courier workflow supports domestic routes within Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Vietnam. Sender and receiver must be in the same supported country. Do not select a different country just to make a rate appear; use an approved alternative delivery arrangement for an unsupported route.

Q-895: Should I submit again if the booking result is uncertain?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
First reload Manual Order List and open the existing order's Details. Check Booking Status, any error, tracking, and waybill information. Do not create a second order or repeat a booking while the first result is uncertain. Use Retry Shipment only when that action is offered for a failed booking, or contact support with the order number.

Q-896: Where can I check why a courier booking failed?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
Go to Manual Order List and open the row's Action menu > Details. Review Booking Status, the booking message, and Status History. Correct the reported address, parcel, service, or wallet problem before using Retry Shipment when it is available. Keep the original order number when contacting support.

Q-897: What is the difference between a payment certificate and a waybill?
Answer: Location: Order Management > Manual Order
A payment certificate is optional evidence of payment; uploading it does not collect money or create a shipping label. A waybill is the courier document used for the shipment. Open certificate and Open Waybill in Details refer to these different documents, so check the file before sending or printing it.

Q-898: Why can I open a page but see Purchase Plan Required when I save?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Store Authorization
Some setup and viewing pages remain available while protected actions require active company access. Check the store's trial or paid-plan days in Store Authorization. If the company has no eligible active store, ask the owner to use Upgrade Plan and complete the required purchase before retrying the action.

Q-899: Does Unlink just hide the store from the list?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Store Authorization
No. Unlink removes the ERP store connection and associated imported product mappings and store-access links. It does not delete the marketplace account itself. Before confirming, check outstanding work and export records you need. Do not use Unlink as a routine refresh or assume reconnecting will preserve every mapping.

Q-900: What do the days selected for Auto Order Accept mean?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Store Authorization
The selected weekdays determine when the ERP's scheduled automatic processing is allowed to run for that store. They are not a number of days to delay each order. Check the store's Auto Order Accept settings and eligible order stage, and review the results in Order Processing instead of assuming every order is immediately accepted.

Q-901: Can any staff member add or delete Sub Accounts?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Account Management > Sub Account
Creating, changing, or deleting Sub Accounts requires the company owner. Seeing Sub Account in the menu does not give a staff member owner authority. Ask the owner to make the account change and review the employee's role, Store Permissions, and Warehouse Permissions.

Q-902: Is Add Account an invitation that the employee accepts later?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Account Management > Sub Account
No. Add Account completes a login check or registration as part of setup and links the user to the company. For an existing login, the matching password is required. Coordinate privately with the employee and choose the correct role, stores, and warehouses; do not expect a separate invitation-acceptance step.

Q-903: Can I add an email that already belongs to another ERP company?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Account Management > Sub Account
Use Add Account only for an employee who is intended to join your company. Linking an existing login can change that user's ERP company membership, so it is not a harmless invitation. Confirm the account and obtain approval from the people responsible for both companies before proceeding; ask support if the membership should remain unchanged.

Q-904: Why can a Sub Account open a page but not see a store or warehouse?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Account Management > Sub Account
Page access and record access are separate. The owner should check the assigned role under Role Management, then edit the Sub Account's Store Permissions and Warehouse Permissions. Confirm the correct store and warehouse are selected and sign in again after the change.

Q-905: Can I make a role read-only by turning off Edit and Delete?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Account Management > Role Management
The current Role Management form uses page-access checkboxes; it does not provide separate Edit and Delete switches for every page. Restrict the pages, stores, and warehouses the employee needs, then verify the actions that remain available. Ask support before relying on the role as a guaranteed read-only account.

Q-906: Why is a role unable to be deleted?
Answer: Location: System Configuration > Account Management > Role Management
The Owner role is protected, and a role assigned to users cannot be removed until those users are reassigned. Have the owner review the affected Sub Accounts, choose suitable replacement roles, and then retry deleting the unused role. Do not remove needed access just to clear a warning.

Q-907: Why is the checkout total higher than the price on one plan?
Answer: Location: Pricing > Purchase subscription
Store subscriptions are charged per selected store. The checkout amount depends on the chosen plan, store count, and currency. Review the selected store names and total before Pay with Stripe. Shipping Wallet top-ups are separate payments and should not be confused with the subscription total.

Q-908: Will renewing early remove the days left on my store plan?
Answer: Location: Pricing > Purchase subscription
A successful renewal adds the purchased period after the later of the existing expiry or the payment date. Remaining valid days are therefore retained. After payment, check the selected store and updated validity in Store Authorization, and contact support with the payment reference if they do not match.

Q-909: Can I add a referral code after I have paid?
Answer: Location: Pricing > Checkout
The customer checkout flow accepts an eligible code before payment; it does not offer a later add-code action on Payment Success. Check the code on Checkout before Pay with Stripe. If you already paid without it, ask support whether anything can be done, without assuming a reward can be added afterward.

Q-910: Who receives the gift when a referral code is used?
Answer: Location: Top-right account menu > My gift
A successful eligible referral creates the gift for the code owner's company, not an automatic free printer for the buyer. The recipient owner can open My gift to review the reward and submit delivery details. The buyer should still check the full subscription amount at Checkout.

Q-911: Does Receive Gift mean I am confirming that the parcel arrived?
Answer: Location: Top-right account menu > My gift
No. Receive Gift on the address form submits your delivery address after confirmation. Confirm Received is the later action for a gift marked Delivered and should be used only after the parcel actually arrives. Read the gift's status before choosing either action.

Q-912: Can I change the default warehouse for only my own login?
Answer: Location: Warehouse Management
The default warehouse is a company-wide setting, not a private preference for one login. Before changing it in Warehouse Management, check with the owner or team responsible for order and stock setup. Always review the warehouse selected in a form even when a default is provided.

Q-913: Why should I check the Sales Trends tooltip before comparing figures?
Answer: Location: Dashboard > Sales Trends
Hover over a graph point to see Sales, Orders, and Qty. The graph can use quantity or order count when positive sales value is unavailable, so a plotted number is not always money. Confirm the platform, month, year, and displayed metric before using the chart for a sales comparison.

Q-914: Can the chatbot change an order or show my exact live stock?
Answer: Location: Floating AI assistant
The assistant explains the documented workflows; it cannot carry out an order change or read your private live inventory. Open the relevant Order Management page or Inventory List to check and change records using your account permissions. Never treat a general help answer as confirmation that an action or payment has completed.

Q-915: What should I do if a help answer names a button I cannot see?
Answer: Location: General support
Check the page and submenu, the selected record's status, and your account access. Some actions appear only in a row's three-dot Action menu or at an eligible stage. If the label or page still differs, send Contact a screenshot with personal details hidden and quote the question number. Do not use a different action just because its name sounds similar.
